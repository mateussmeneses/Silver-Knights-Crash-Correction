## What to look for

- A `call [CreateMailslotA]` or `call [CreateFileA]` near the launcher format string
  identifies the leaking open.
- A matching `call [CloseHandle]` dominated by it would mean the handle IS closed and the
  leak has another cause; its ABSENCE within the same function supports the reopen theory.
- The peer channel's site should hold its handle in a global and never re-open it. That
  difference is the model for the fix.
