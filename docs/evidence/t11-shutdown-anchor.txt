## Next step (not done by this tool)

1. Take the enclosing function's start address for the reference above.
2. Find its CALLERS with `tools/disasm_exports.py`-style xref scanning, or in REA:
     mcp__rea__procedure_callers at that address
3. Walk up until a caller is guarded by a time comparison. That caller is the cap.

This is the positive route. A byte search for 1800000 was already tried and found
nothing, which is itself a useful negative - see
docs/evidence/t11-time-cap-search-analysis.txt.
